# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s risk summary email and the highlighted risks
- **Value proposition:** Highlight the top risks associated with customer interaction cross-channel and place it into 3 buckets of work based on high,med,low focusing on what I need to act on, each with a source I can check.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each risk come from something that actually happened in the source material identified and does it reference that channel? | Yes / No | At least one risk is invented or can’t be traced back to the channel and original correspondence. | n/a | Every risk traces to a real message, ticket, or doc. |
| Sources | Does every risk link to the message or ticket it came from? | Yes / No | One or more risks has no source, or the link is wrong. | n/a | Every risk has a working link to its source. |
| Prioritization | Are the risks the ones that most deserve my attention this week or were any miscategorized as high?  | Yes / No | Miscategorized some as high. | n/a | Reflects all risks accurately. |
| Usefulness | Could I act on this summary without rereading the sources? | Yes / No | I do not understand the risk or the risk is improperly identified. | n/a | The summary clearly defines whether I need to act on it or not and what the risk is. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No names, salaries, or customer details beyond what’s needed. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 2 summaries a week, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization and usefulness, and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
