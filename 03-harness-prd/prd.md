# Harness Sketch

Describe Juno's role and task: Juno takes insights cross channels including social media, email, slack tickets, and any phone data to draft specs that unblock delivery, flag risks proactively, and highlight the biggest focus customers at risk of being lost. It sends a daily email to the PM to show outreach drafts and recommended options.

_Rough first pass, Module 3 Lab 1. Not the AI PRD._

## 01 · Context
Product strategy one pager, slack, tickets, any call logs, any email threads.

## 02 · Tools
Draft a ranked list and email it to the PM.

## 03 · Loop
One pass per batch of signals

## 04 · Memory
Stores log of high risk clients to see if they pop up again.

## 05 · Permissions
Nothing gets sent to clients for outreach unless reviewed and approved by the PM.

## 06 · Verification
Pm confirms whether a draft is good to send and approves it.
